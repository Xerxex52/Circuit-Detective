TERMS OF USE

The terms, simply.

Useful information about buying and using the game.

Updated 20 September 2026

Application licence

Circuit Detective is distributed through the App Store. Apple’s standard application licence agreement governs its use. This page explains the product and does not replace that agreement.

One purchase

The game includes 72 progressive missions, an optional soldering challenge, the free workshop, instruments, lessons and circuit building. There are no subscriptions, ads or in-app purchases. The applicable price is the one shown in your App Store at purchase; the planned price in France is €7.99.

Apple handles payment, downloading and any refund requests. For purchase questions, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

An educational simulation

Components and measurements are modelled for learning and experimenting in the game. Circuit Detective is not a tool for certifying or validating a physical circuit.

Operation and local storage

The app works offline, without an account, on iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 26 or later. Progress and circuits stay on your device. There is no built-in cross-device sync. Deleting the app may cause local data to be lost.
