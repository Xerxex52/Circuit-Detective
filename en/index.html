YOUR FIRST LIGHTBULB MOMENT

This LED needs
the right resistor.

The battery supplies 3 V. The LED uses 2 V and needs a current of 10 mA. Which resistor will light it at the intended current?

Need a hint?+

There is 1 V across the resistor: 3 V − 2 V. Ohm’s law gives R = V ÷ I. Remember to convert 10 mA to 0.01 A.

This small experiment illustrates the idea. In the game, you handle the circuits, consult datasheets and take your own measurements.

BEFORE YOU STEP INSIDE

Good questions.

Do I need to know electronics already?+

You start with resistors, LEDs and your first measurements. Lessons, datasheets and hints help along the way. Later missions gradually require more reasoning and calculation.

How are missions different from the workshop?+

Missions give you a specific objective and unlock as you progress. Your workshop lets you explore the tools, practise and build your own circuits on a virtual breadboard.

What does the purchase include?+

The full game: all 73 missions, the workshop, instruments, lessons, datasheets and calculator. No in-app purchase or subscription is required to unlock these features. The price in France is €7.99; check the App Store for pricing in your region.

Do I need equipment or an internet connection?+

No physical electronics equipment is needed. After downloading, the game works offline without an account. It is an educational simulation: components and measurements are modelled for learning.

Which devices are supported?+

Circuit Detective is designed for iPhone and iPad running iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later. The game is available in English and French.

Does my progress sync between devices?+

Progress, settings and circuits are stored locally. Progress does not sync between devices. If you run into a problem, contact support before deleting the app.
