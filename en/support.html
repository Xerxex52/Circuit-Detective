SUPPORT

A little help at the workbench.

Get started, find a tool or tell us about a problem.

Updated 20 September 2026

Start playing

  1. Follow the tour with Next, then Start, or close it with the cross. Menus shown during the tour are previews.
  2. Read the first mission objective. Datasheets, Lessons and Hints help you find the solution.
  3. Levels shows your progress. Workbench opens the free experimentation area, available from the start.

Find your way around

Drag with one finger to rotate the 3D scene; use two fingers to pan. Pinch to zoom. View board brings the circuit closer; Solder side shows its underside. Use the View menu to return to a preset viewpoint.

In the workbench, My circuit opens the breadboard. The Workbench menu offers a blank board and editable examples. Choose Inspect, then tap a component to change it.

Landscape gives circuits more room on iPhone. The breadboard also has a portrait layout. In the editor, use two fingers to zoom and pan.

Guides, language and text size

Open Settings using the gear, or from More in the workbench. Change the language or text size, reopen the Complete guide or replay the Interactive tour.

Progress and saved circuits

The game works offline without an account. Settings, progress and circuits stay on your device. There is no cross-device sync. Contact us before deleting the app if you want to preserve your progress.
