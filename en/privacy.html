Privacy policy

Developer: Soheil RAMEZANI.

Circuit Detective does not collect or transmit personal data. The game works offline, without an account, ads, advertising tracking or third-party analytics.

Your progress, settings and circuits are stored on your device. They are not sent to the developer. They may be included in device backups managed by Apple, depending on your settings.

Deleting the app removes its local data. A device backup may retain a copy. Circuit Detective does not sync progress between devices.

Apple handles payment, downloads and, depending on your choices, system and TestFlight diagnostics under its own privacy policies.

If you contact support by email, your address and the information you send are used only to handle your request. They are not sold or used for advertising.

Privacy questions: sramezani52@gmail.com.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection data under its own privacy statement. This website adds no audience analytics or advertising trackers. GitHub Privacy Statement.
